# Companion character renders

The apricot and cream companions are adapted from **Autumn**, the dog character
from the Blender Studio open movie *Spring*.

**Autumn / Spring © Blender Foundation | [Blender Studio](https://studio.blender.org/characters/autumn/v1/)**

Source license: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The [Spring licensing page](https://studio.blender.org/projects/spring/pages/about/)
confirms that its published production data shares this license. The character
download page currently contains an apparent copied “Einar Rig” credit; Autumn
and its originating Spring production are identified here explicitly.

Changes for this birthday card: warm apricot and cream coat colour, dropped ears,
relaxed brow and eyelids, adjusted canine eye materials, softly curled groom,
portrait lighting, and original idle, gaze, petting, celebration and resting
animation. These are adaptations of Autumn's existing mesh, groom and rig; they
are not Disney assets or a newly sculpted anatomically exact poodle.

Generated using the free Blender 4.5 LTS and FFmpeg toolchain. The browser assets
are 24 fps H.264 clips with colour and opacity stored side by side; a shader
reconstructs transparency. Transparent WebP posters provide a still fallback.

Reproduction scripts: `scripts/cinema/build_pets.py` and
`scripts/cinema/encode_pets.py` in the source repository. Editable `.blend` files
and full-resolution RGBA frame sequences stay in the local `.asset-build` folder.
